## Project Details: 
This project brings the player into a Volleyball Gym, and challenges them to complete 4 puzzles to escape! 

**Puzzle #1**: Serve at the targets; pick up the volleyball off the rack on along the south wall and throw it at the targets on the other side of the net. Even bouncing the ball off the wall and hitting the target will count.

**Puzzle #2**: Get into rotation; Arrange the jerseys on the North side of the court (closer to the scoreboard) in the correct order to get into Rotation 1, with your setter serving the ball. Dropping the jerseys in the wrong spots on the court will play an incorrect sound to tell you that you've got the wrong arrangement. 

**Puzzle #3**: Serve Receive; stand on the white circle on the south side of the court, and press the triggers on both of your controllers simultaniously to pass the ball being served at you into the orange target hoop 3 times. 

**Puzzle #4**: Key Pad; Once you've done the other 3 puzzles, go to the door and find the key pad. Check the score on the game displayed on the scoreboard and input that 4 digit code into the keypad to escape! 

**Game elements**: The user is able to move around smoothly using their left joystick and teleport around with their right joystick. The user is racing the clock to complete all of the puzzles and escape in under 5 minutes. The fastest escape times are also logged in a **Local Leaderboard** stored locally on the quest.
